## Overview
The Zomi people are an ethnic minority from the Chin State of Myanmar. They are among the many ethnic groups that have fled Myanmar seeking refuge in Malaysia. My master's research project examined how Zomi refugees adapted psychologically to life in Malaysia — specifically, how they found a sense of well-being and life satisfaction after a period of cross-cultural transition in a new country.

I looked at how several psychological factors shape Zomi refugees' subjective well-being: loss of control, perceptions of forced displacement, the dangers associated with that displacement, and perceived discrimination in the receiving society. I also compared Zomi refugees with non-Zomi refugees to see whether their experiences differed.

The project resulted in a master's thesis, a conference paper, and several community talks where I shared the findings back with the Zomi community in Malaysia. Most importantly, it led to a Master of Psychology in Intercultural Relations.
