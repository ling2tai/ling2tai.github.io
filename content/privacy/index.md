### 3.1. Server Logs (Automated)
When you visit this site, my web hosting provider (GitHub Pages) automatically records standard server log data. This includes:
*   Your IP address
*   Browser type and version
*   Time and date of the visit
*   Referrer URL

**Purpose:** These logs are used solely for security monitoring and preventing abuse. They are not linked to your personal identity and are typically anonymized or deleted after a short period.

### 3.2. Embedded Content (YouTube & Canva)
This website contains embedded content from third-party services. When you view a page with embedded content, these services may collect data about you:

*   **YouTube (Google):** Videos are embedded using YouTube's standard player. When you load a page with a YouTube video, a connection is established to Google's servers in the US. Google may receive your IP address and browser information. If you are logged into your Google account while viewing the video, Google may associate your browsing activity with your account.
    *   *Privacy Impact:* Google may use this data for analytics and advertising purposes.
    *   *Control:* You can prevent this by logging out of your Google account before visiting this site or using browser extensions that block trackers. Refer to [YouTube's Privacy Policy](https://www.youtube.com/howyoutubeworks/privacy/) for more details.

*   **Canva:** Design elements and presentations are embedded via Canva. Loading these elements establishes a connection to Canva's servers. Canva may receive your IP address and information about your interaction with the embedded content.
    *   *Privacy Impact:* Canva may use this data to improve its services and for analytics.
    *   *Control:* Refer to [Canva's Privacy Policy](https://www.canva.com/policies/privacy-policy/) for more details.

**Note on Cookies:** While this site itself does not set cookies, these third-party embeds may set their own cookies or use local storage depending on your browser settings and account status with those services.
